How it works

  • Move Drag a photo or the text anywhere on the card.
  • Resize Drag the corner handle at the bottom right.
  • 2:3
    Aspect ratio Tap the pill at the top left of a photo to switch between 2:3 and 3:2.
  • Tilt Tip a photo a few degrees left or right with the buttons at its bottom edge. Tap the angle between them to straighten it.
  • Crop Opens crop mode: drag to pan the photo inside its frame, pinch or scroll (or use −/+) to zoom. Double-tap a photo for the same. Tap the check to finish.
  • Text Tap the pencil (or double-tap the text) to edit it. Enter finishes, Shift+Enter starts a new line.
  • Delete The red button, or Del on a keyboard.
  • Keyboard Arrow keys nudge the selected element (Shift for larger steps); Esc leaves crop mode or deselects.
  • Safety area The dotted line shows where the print is trimmed: about 6 mm at every edge is cut off, so keep faces and text inside it.
